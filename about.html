About

We believe God reveals Himself through His Word, by His Spirit, and for His glory.

Our prayer is that, as you read, watch, or listen to the content we produce, God would reveal more of Himself to you.

God bless you.

Meet the GRG Team

Pierce Gorman

Founder

Ever since Pierce got saved at 18 years old, his passion has been to study and teach the Word of God. God Revealing God is an overflow of this passion that God instilled in him. He believes that God has and does reveal Himself to His people, and it's Pierce's mission to show all people all the ways that God reveals Himself (don't worry, he knows it's an impossible task).

Pierce and his wife Hannah have one newborn son, Ezra Shepherd.

His hobbies include reading, writing, running, and camping/hiking.

Favorite verse: Romans 11:36
